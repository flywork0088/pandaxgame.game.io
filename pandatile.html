5. App Tracking Transparency on iOS

On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework may ask for your permission before the app or advertising partners access the IDFA or track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies.

If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use the IDFA and related ad interaction data to deliver more relevant ads and measure ad performance. If you deny tracking, the game remains playable, but ads may be less relevant and certain advertising measurement features may be limited.

You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

ThreeTiles Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 3, 2026
App: ThreeTiles
Bundle ID: com.jsvcgame.threetiles.ios

This Privacy Policy explains how ThreeTiles ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app. ThreeTiles is operated by JVGCGameStudio.

Before publishing this page, confirm the support email and hosted URL used in App Store Connect.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Device and app identifiers, such as advertising identifiers, app instance identifiers, device model, operating system, language, country or region, and IP-derived approximate location.
  • Gameplay and product interaction data, such as level progress, in-game currency changes, item usage, store views, ad interactions, and other app events.
  • Purchase information, such as product identifiers, purchase status, transaction identifiers, receipt data, price, and currency.
  • Advertising data, such as ad requests, ad impressions, ad clicks, ad revenue, mediation network, ad placement, and attribution data.
  • Diagnostics and stability data, such as crash reports, error logs, performance data, and device state at the time of an issue.

How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • To operate the app, deliver in-app purchases, restore eligible purchases, and provide rewarded ad benefits.
  • To measure gameplay, purchase, advertising, and app performance.
  • To show ads, measure ad effectiveness, attribute installs and events, and improve monetization.
  • To detect fraud, prevent abuse, debug errors, and improve app stability.
  • To comply with platform, legal, accounting, and App Store requirements.

Advertising And Tracking

ThreeTiles may use advertising and attribution technologies to deliver ads and measure ad performance. On iOS, the app may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using data for tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies. You can allow or deny tracking in iOS Settings at any time.

In-App Purchases

ThreeTiles offers consumable in-app purchases for coins, item bundles, sale bundles, and PiggyBank, and one non-consumable purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive purchase-related information needed to deliver items, validate receipts, restore eligible non-consumable purchases, and measure purchase events.

Third-Party Services

ThreeTiles uses third-party services that may collect and process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit / Unity IAP for purchase processing and receipt validation.
  • AppLovin MAX and supported ad network adapters, including AdMob, for ad mediation and advertising.
  • AppsFlyer for attribution, analytics, purchase validation, and ad revenue measurement.
  • Firebase Analytics for app analytics and event measurement.
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and diagnostics, if enabled in the build.

You can review these providers' policies here:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AppsFlyer Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Unity Privacy Policy

Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, provide advertising, analytics, attribution, purchase validation, crash reporting, and fraud prevention. We may also share information when required by law, platform rules, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the app, or others.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including app operation, analytics, advertising measurement, purchase records, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and dispute resolution. Third-party services may retain data according to their own policies.

Children's Privacy

ThreeTiles is not directed to children under the age of 13 or the minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Your Choices

  • You can manage app tracking permission in iOS Settings.
  • You can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier through device settings where available.
  • You can request deletion or review of information by contacting us.
  • You can uninstall the app to stop future data collection by the app.

International Transfers

Information may be processed in countries or regions other than where you live. These locations may have different data protection laws. We and our service providers take measures designed to protect information as described in this policy.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and make the updated policy available through the app or App Store listing.
